The studio

Two people. One variable:
make it worth playing.

Blue Variable is a two-person independent studio. Small on purpose — every mechanic, sprite, and sound gets our full attention, and nothing ships until it feels right in the hands.

Carefully crafted

We build slowly and deliberately. Prototype, play, throw away, rebuild — until the game underneath the idea finally shows up.

Games we’d love to play

Our only pitch filter. If we wouldn’t clear an evening for it ourselves, it doesn’t get made.

Purrfect Fit

A cozy puzzle game about cats and the spaces they are absolutely certain they fit into. Nudge, rotate, and stack your way to feline approval — one impossibly snug spot at a time.

  • Nudge, rotate, and stack cats into impossibly snug spots
  • Every cat has opinions about where it fits
  • Cozy by design — soft pace, short sessions

Purrfect Fit isn’t on Steam yet. Follow the devlog and you’ll be the first to know when the store page goes live.
