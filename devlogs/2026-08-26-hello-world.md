# Hello, world — meet Blue Variable Studio

We're Blue Variable Studio: a two-person independent game studio. Our whole philosophy fits in one sentence — *games we'd love to play, carefully crafted into existence.*

Being two people means every pixel, every sound, and every line of code passes through hands that care about it. It also means we get to stay small, stay weird, and take the time a game actually needs.

## What we're making

Our first game is **Purrfect Fit** — an upcoming cozy puzzle game about cats and the spaces they insist on occupying. If a cat has ever claimed a box, a bowl, or a slightly-too-small sunbeam in your home, you already understand the design document.

It's not on Steam *yet*. The store page is coming — when it's live, this devlog is where you'll hear it first.

## Why a devlog?

A few reasons:

- Building in the open keeps us honest.
- The messy middle of game development is the most interesting part, and it usually goes undocumented.
- Someday we'll want to remember how this all happened.

Expect posts about design decisions, art experiments, technical rabbit holes, and the occasional cat reference photo used strictly for research purposes.
